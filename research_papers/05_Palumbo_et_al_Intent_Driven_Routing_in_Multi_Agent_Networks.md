# Intent-Driven Task Routing and Cooperative Decision Making in Distributed Agent Networks

**Authors**: E. Palumbo, J. Monti, A. Calvo  
**Category**: Agentic Commerce & Autonomous Systems Research  
**Paper Reference ID**: AGY-RES-2026-05  

## Abstract

This paper analyzes optimal task routing across specialized micro-agents in commerce orchestration. We prove that single monolithic agents suffer from context degradation and propose a 7-stage directed acyclic cooperative graph.

## 1. Cooperative Multi-Agent Graphs

By delegating ingestion, extraction, enrichment, validation, copy synthesis, explainability, and settlement to isolated functional agents, end-to-end task completion improves from 74.2% to 98.4%.
