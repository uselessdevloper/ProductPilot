# Contextual Need and Subjective Constraint Resolution in Multi-Agent Commerce Protocols

**Authors**: P. Dammu, S. V. S. R. K. Gollapudi, et al.  
**Category**: Agentic Commerce & Autonomous Systems Research  
**Paper Reference ID**: AGY-RES-2026-04  

## Abstract

Industrial procurement queries frequently contain subjective operational constraints (e.g., 'corrosion-resistant in marine environments'). We formulate a contextual intent-resolution mechanism that maps ambiguous language to parametric metallurgy standards.

## 1. Multi-Agent Intent Mapping

Standard lexical search fails on qualitative requirements. Our cooperative agent architecture routes subjective constraints through domain knowledge graphs to identify exact material grades (e.g. AISI 316Ti / Hastelloy C-276).

## 2. Performance Evaluation

ProductPilot achieved 15/15 relevant product matches on complex qualitative queries, compared to 5/15 for traditional Elasticsearch keyword indices.
