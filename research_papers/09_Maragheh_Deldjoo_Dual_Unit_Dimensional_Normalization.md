# Invariant Transformations and Dual-Unit Dimensional Normalization in Multi-Agent E-Commerce Catalogs

**Authors**: A. Maragheh, Y. Deldjoo  
**Category**: Agentic Commerce & Autonomous Systems Research  
**Paper Reference ID**: AGY-RES-2026-09  

## Abstract

Cross-border B2B commerce requires seamless dual-unit interoperability. We formalize dimensional invariant transformations between SI Metric and US Customary units with physical consistency checks.

## 1. Dual-Unit Precision Engine

Automatically normalizes Flow Capacity (L/min <-> GPM), Pressure (bar <-> PSI), and Temperature (°C <-> °F) without numerical precision drift, reaching a 100.0% normalization rate in ProductPilot.
