# Persona-Aligned Synthetic Buyer Modeling and Multi-Attribute Knowledge Graph Construction

**Authors**: Y. Mansour, M. Schapira, G. Valiant  
**Category**: Agentic Commerce & Autonomous Systems Research  
**Paper Reference ID**: AGY-RES-2026-06  

## Abstract

We introduce synthetic buyer personas to simulate enterprise procurement behaviors and stress-test automated pricing guardrails under varied risk tolerances.

## 1. Persona-Driven Simulation

Simulated procurement personas (Plant Operations Lead, Compliance Officer, Cost Optimizer) validate that catalog schemas satisfy diverse operational criteria.
