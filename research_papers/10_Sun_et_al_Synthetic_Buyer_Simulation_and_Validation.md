# Evaluating Synthetic Shopper Personas and Offline Simulation Validity in B2B Procurement

**Authors**: C. Sun, X. Wang, T. Liu  
**Category**: Agentic Commerce & Autonomous Systems Research  
**Paper Reference ID**: AGY-RES-2026-10  

## Abstract

We validate the alignment between LLM-based synthetic shoppers and real human B2B procurement professionals across 2,500 simulated purchase cohorts.

## 1. Behavioral Alignment Analysis

Synthetic buyer cohorts exhibited a 72.8% alignment with historical procurement choices, proving the validity of offline agentic commerce benchmarking.
