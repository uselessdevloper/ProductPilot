# Augmented Retrieval-Augmented Generation (ARAG) for Parametric Catalog Enrichment in Enterprise Retail

**Authors**: Walmart Global Tech Labs  
**Category**: Agentic Commerce & Autonomous Systems Research  
**Paper Reference ID**: AGY-RES-2026-07  

## Abstract

We detail an enterprise-scale ARAG architecture that fills incomplete catalog attributes by retrieving grounded parameters from secondary engineering manuals, safety certifications, and manufacturer repositories.

## 1. Missing Attribute Imputation

Industrial products often arrive with null specifications in ERP dumps. ARAG queries secondary technical repositories with strict factual grounding to achieve complete ETIM 8.0 taxonomy coverage.
